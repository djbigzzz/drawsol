DrawSol

A prize draw with a free entry route, on Solana, built for the Colosseum hackathon. Devnet demo · play money. DrawSol is run for a house share, set in each draw’s account and enforced by the program: 50–60% of a pot draw’s ticket money, and at least that at a headline draw’s sell-out. Every number on this page is read from Solana devnet; when it can’t be read, it isn’t shown.
